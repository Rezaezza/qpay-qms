Overview

Qpay ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Qpay decentralized application ("Platform").

Because Qpay is a non-custodial blockchain application, we fundamentally do not have access to your funds, private keys, or wallet credentials. Transactions occur directly on the QMS blockchain and are publicly verifiable by anyone.

✅
Privacy by design: Qpay's architecture minimizes data collection by design. Your wallet interacts directly with smart contracts on QMS Testnet. We serve only the frontend interface — we are not a financial intermediary and do not process payments on your behalf.

3

Blockchain Data & Public Transparency

All transactions on QMS Testnet are publicly recorded on the blockchain and are visible to anyone with access to the network. This includes:

  • Your wallet address (sender and recipient)
  • Transaction amounts and token types
  • Smart contract interactions (payroll creation, payments, swaps)
  • Employee wallet addresses and payroll amounts entered into the QpayPayroll contract
  • Swap records stored in the QpaySwap contract
  • Timestamps and block numbers of all transactions
🔍
Blockchain transparency: By design, all data stored in smart contracts is publicly readable by anyone. Do not store sensitive personal information (names, identification numbers, etc.) in any field that gets recorded on-chain. Employee names entered in Qpay are stored permanently on the QMS blockchain and are publicly visible.

This is an inherent property of public blockchains and is not within Qpay's control. Once data is written to the blockchain, it cannot be deleted or modified.

4

How We Use Data

Since we do not collect personal data beyond what is publicly available on the blockchain, our data usage is extremely limited:

  • Displaying your wallet's on-chain data within the Qpay interface (payrolls, balances, swap history)
  • Reading public blockchain state to power the application's features
  • No marketing, profiling, advertising, or data monetization of any kind

We do not build user profiles, sell data to third parties, or use your information for any purpose beyond providing the core service.

5

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Public blockchain data — transaction data is inherently public on QMS Testnet and accessible to anyone
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • Protection of rights — we may share information to protect the rights, property, or safety of Qpay, its users, or the public

The QWAP DEX protocol and QMS blockchain infrastructure are third-party services. When you interact with them through Qpay, their own terms and privacy practices apply.

6

Cookies & Local Storage

Qpay does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

Your web browser may use local storage or session storage for:

  • Wallet connection state (managed by ConnectKit / wagmi — these are cleared when you disconnect)
  • User interface preferences such as selected network or last-used settings
✅
No transaction data in local storage: All payroll records, payment history, and swap history are stored exclusively on the QMS blockchain — not in your browser's local storage. Your data is always available from any device.

You can clear browser local storage at any time through your browser settings without affecting your on-chain data.

7

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the Qpay frontend and smart contracts:

  • Smart contracts have been internally reviewed for common vulnerabilities
  • The frontend application does not store or transmit private keys
  • All wallet interactions are handled by the user's own wallet software (e.g., MetaMask)
  • HTTPS is used for all frontend communications
  • Smart contract code is publicly verifiable on the QMS block explorer

However, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own wallet, private keys, and connected devices. We strongly recommend using a hardware wallet for significant real-world value transactions (on mainnet).

8

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — you can view all data associated with your wallet directly on the QMS blockchain explorer
  • Right to deletion — blockchain data is immutable and cannot be deleted; this is a fundamental property of decentralized networks
  • Right to portability — your data is publicly accessible on the blockchain and can be exported via any blockchain data tool
  • Right to object — you may stop using Qpay at any time; disconnecting your wallet ceases all interaction with our platform

Since we collect minimal off-chain data, most traditional data rights (access, correction, deletion of server-side records) do not apply in the traditional sense — your primary data exists on the public blockchain.

Third-Party Services

Qpay interacts with the following third-party services and protocols:

  • QMS Blockchain Network — the underlying blockchain infrastructure. All transactions are broadcast to and recorded on this network.
  • QWAP DEX — the decentralized exchange protocol used for token swaps. Swap transactions are executed via QWAP's smart contracts.
  • MetaMask / Web3 Wallets — wallet software used to sign transactions. These have their own privacy policies.
  • QMS Scan — the block explorer for QMS Testnet, used for verifying transactions.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.
