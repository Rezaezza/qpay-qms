Service Description

Qpay is a non-custodial, decentralized payroll and payment platform built on the QMS blockchain network. The Platform provides the following services:

  • On-chain payroll management — create, manage, and execute payroll entries recorded on QMS Testnet
  • Stablecoin payments — send and receive USDT and USDC tokens directly from your connected wallet
  • Token swaps — exchange tokens via the QWAP decentralized exchange (Uniswap V2-compatible)
  • Transaction history — view on-chain records of all payroll payments and token swaps
  • Batch payments — pay multiple employees in a single blockchain transaction

Qpay is a frontend interface that interacts with smart contracts deployed on QMS Testnet. We do not hold, custody, or control any user funds or private keys at any time.

Non-custodial: All transactions are executed directly from your connected wallet. Qpay never has access to your private keys or funds. You maintain full control and responsibility over your assets.
